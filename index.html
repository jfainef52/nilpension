Go pro or not, every athletic career ends someday. And most careers after sports don’t come with a pension.

How it works

Fund it once. Let it work.

The NIL Pension is built on a life insurance policy designed to build cash value and pay income later. Three stages, one simple idea.

In college

Fund it

Set aside a small portion of NIL or revenue-share income. That’s the only money you put in.

Through your career

Let it grow

Nothing more out of pocket. Your policy does the work while your career and life move forward.

When you need it

Take income

Optional income, potentially tax-free. You choose when to start, and it can last through retirement.

Who is this for?

Build something that outlasts your career

  • Use a fraction of your NIL income, not all of it.
  • It’s one more asset, built while you’re still in college. It doesn’t depend on how long your playing career lasts.
  • Income later in life is optional and potentially tax-free.
  • Talk it over with someone you trust and a tax professional first.

Why I do this

Jeff Faine, CLU

Founder, Integrated Planning Partners

I’m a Notre Dame alumnus and a former NFL offensive lineman, with stops with the Cleveland Browns, New Orleans Saints, Tampa Bay Buccaneers and Cincinnati Bengals.

On the offensive line, my job was to protect others and create opportunities for them. That’s the reason I entered financial services in the first place.

It extends to the athletes we work with. The work they do on the court and the field should pay off for them long after their playing days have ended. NIL Pension is one way we make that happen.

I’m also the author of The Architecture of Retirement and co-founder of The Faine House, a nonprofit supporting young adults aging out of foster care. NIL Pension is a solution of Integrated Planning Partners, the firm I founded.
